What I Actually Do

Think of me as the person between the idea and the execution.

Companies hire developer community managers and program managers when things are too complex for one team to handle alone. I'm the one who builds the community, figures out the plan, aligns everyone, and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

I turn chaos into a plan

When a project involves multiple teams, vendors, and timelines, I define the strategy, set up governance, and make sure everyone knows what's happening and when.

I build systems, not spreadsheets

I automate the boring stuff — partner onboarding, vendor payments, reporting pipelines — so teams can focus on the work that matters instead of chasing status updates.

I grow things that need growing

Whether it's a developer community, an education platform, or an adoption program — I figure out what's blocking growth and systematically remove those barriers.
