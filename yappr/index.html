how it works

Three moves. Zero typing.

01

Hold Right ⌥

In any app, in any text box. Or double-tap ⌘ to go hands-free.

02

Say it messy

Ums, restarts, “no wait, 4pm”. Talk like you talk. Yappr keeps up.

03

Let go

Clean text lands at your cursor, shaped for the app you're in. Your clipboard goes back to how it was.

features

It gets you.
Like, actually.

Not just speech-to-text. Yappr cleans up how people really talk, then writes it the way you'd write it.

Writes for the room

Emails get a greeting and sign-off. Chats stay short. Terminals get literal commands. Alpie Seed reads the room for you.

Gets your take-backs

Drops the ums and uhs. Hears “at 3, no wait, 4pm” and writes 4pm. Follows “new line” and “scratch that”.

Punctuates by vibe

Your voice goes up at the end, you get a question mark. Short pause, comma. Long pause, new sentence.

Learns your fixes

Fix a misheard name once, like “DJ Mike” to “DJI Mic”, and it's right from then on.

Tunes out the room

Music, the TV, someone across the room. Yappr focuses on your voice and ignores what your Mac is playing.

Audio stays home

Speech is turned into text on your Mac by Apple's on-device model. Your voice is never uploaded or saved.

privacy

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

No audio uploads. No recordings saved. Here's exactly what goes where.

audio

Turned into text on your Mac by Apple's speech model. Never sent anywhere, never saved.

text

Only the words go to Alpie Seed, 169Pi's model, for a tidy-up, with the app name, the window title and anything you've told Yappr to use, like your word list and sign-off. When you fix a dictation, your edit goes too, so Alpie can learn from it. The text around your cursor stays put unless you turn that on.

offline

Want zero cloud? Switch to basic cleanup. Everything stays on your Mac.

faq

Fair questions.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

What permissions does it need?

Microphone, so it can hear you. Accessibility, so it can paste where your cursor is. The mic only opens while you dictate, unless you ask Yappr to keep a slow-to-wake mic ready.

What if nothing's selected when I talk?

Your words go on the clipboard with a nudge to press ⌘V wherever you want them.
